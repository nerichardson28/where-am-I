What Changed?



Once a European settlement was established, the name of the area changed, it was no longer Third Crossing but known as Palestine. This name lasted from 1871, when Treaty 2 was first signed, to 1879 when the name was changed again to Gladstone after the Canadian Prime Minister William Ewart Gladstone.

Once the settlement was established the area changed from the Indigenous way of life to more of a European way such as homesteads, agriculture, farming animals, schools, businesses and railroads.
