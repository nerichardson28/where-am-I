What is happening there now?



Unfortunately, there is not alot to report about Gladstone these days. It is a very sleepy little community still largely based on agriculture. There is still hunting and fishing done in the area but it is mostly deer and pickrel and done mostly for sport and not a way of survival.

As for the relationship with the Indigenous people that is pretty much none existant now as the community is mostly made up of the descendants of the European settlers and a growing community of Filipino people.

As a personal note, I wish there was still an indigenous population. The area could use a dose of the original culture that was seen in the area before Gladstone was established.
